    FAQ

    Which formats are available?

    SRT works with almost every video player and editor. VTT is the web standard used by HTML5 players. TXT is a clean transcript without timestamps.

    What do Original, Auto-generated and Translated mean?

    Original subtitles were uploaded by the creator. Auto-generated are speech-recognition captions. Translated are machine translations of the captions provided by the platform.

    Why does a video show no subtitles?

    The video may have none, may be private or age-restricted, or the site may require you to be signed in. See the README for using browser cookies.

    Is anything uploaded to a server?

    No. Everything runs on your own computer.

      How it works

      1. Copy the link of the video page.

      3. Pick a language and download as SRT, word-by-word SRT, VTT or TXT.

      FAQ

      Which formats are available?

      SRT works with almost every video player and editor. Words is an SRT with one word per subtitle — great for animated captions and editing (exact timings for auto-generated captions, estimated for others). VTT is the web standard used by HTML5 players. TXT is a clean transcript without timestamps.

      What do Original, Auto-generated and Translated mean?

      Original subtitles were uploaded by the creator. Auto-generated are speech-recognition captions. Translated are machine translations of the captions provided by the platform.

      How does bulk download work?

      Switch to Bulk links, paste many links (one per line) or upload a .txt/.csv file, choose language and format, and press start. Each video is processed in turn and you get one ZIP with all subtitle files.

      Why does a video show no subtitles?

      The video may have none, may be private or age-restricted, or the site may require you to be signed in. See the README for using browser cookies.

      Is anything uploaded to a server?

      No. Everything runs on your own computer.

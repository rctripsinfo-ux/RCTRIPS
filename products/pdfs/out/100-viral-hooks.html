Pehle ye samjho

Hook = pehle 3 second

Reel ka 80% kaam pehli line karti hai. Viewer 1–2 second mein decide karta hai ki rukna hai ya scroll karna hai.

Achha hook teen kaam karta hai:
1. Kisi ek insaan ko target karta hai
2. Ek sawaal ya takleef kholta hai
3. Jawab video ke andar chhupata hai

Formula

Har hook ka formula

  • 01Kaun? Kisse baat kar rahe ho: student, creator, naukri wale…
  • 02Kya dard? Unki ek takleef ya chahat
  • 03Kya twist? Ek ulti baat, number, ya raaz
  • 04Kab tak? Jawab aakhir mein, taaki video poori dekhi jaaye
Example: "Padhte ho, par exam mein sab bhool jaate ho? (dard) Problem yaad karne mein nahi, revise karne ke tareeke mein hai. (twist)"

Script templates

Templates 1–4

Intro

HOOK: Agar aap [niche] mein [problem] se pareshaan ho, toh ye page aapke liye hai. BODY: Yahan roz milega: [1], [2], [3]. CTA: Follow karo, kal se shuru.

List

HOOK: [N] [cheezein] jo har [audience] ko pata honi chahiye. BODY: 1… 2… 3… (har point 1 line + 1 example) CTA: Save karo, kaam aayega.

Myth

HOOK: Sabse bada jhooth: '[myth]'. BODY: Log kyun maante hain → asli sach → kya karna chahiye. CTA: Agree karte ho? Comment karo.

Story

HOOK: Ek [insaan] tha jo [problem] se pareshaan tha. BODY: Kya hua → mod kya aaya → kya seekha. CTA: Ye lesson kisi ko bhejo.

Script templates

Templates 5–8

Tip

HOOK: Bas 1 cheez badlo, [result] khud dikhega. BODY: Tip → kaise karna hai (2 steps) → kyun kaam karta hai. CTA: Aaj try karke batao.

Question

HOOK: Sach batao, aap [A] ho ya [B]? BODY: A wale aisa karte hain… B wale aisa… CTA: Comment mein A ya B likho.

Recap

HOOK: Is hafte [N] baatein seekhi. Kitni yaad hain? BODY: 1 line mein har ek. CTA: Save karo + follow, agla hafta aur bhi.

Pain

HOOK: [Pain] ho raha hai? Galti aapki nahi, tareeke ki hai. BODY: Asli wajah → seedha hal → chhota example. CTA: Agar ye aap ho, toh comment mein 'haan'.

Script templates

Templates 9–12

Comparison

HOOK: [A] vs [B]: farak sunke hairaan ho jaoge. BODY: A ka result… B ka result… asli farak. CTA: Aap kaunse ho? Batao.

Secret

HOOK: Ye baat bahut kam log jaante hain. BODY: Raaz → kaise use karein → ek example. CTA: Save karo, dobara nahi milega.

Kadwa sach

HOOK: Kadwa sach: [statement]. BODY: Kyun ye sach hai → kya karna chahiye. CTA: Sehmat ho? Share karo.

Challenge

HOOK: 7 din ka challenge, karoge? BODY: Roz kya karna hai → kya farak aayega. CTA: Comment mein 'IN' likho.

Example

Template se poori script

Niche: Money tips · Format: Myth

HOOK: Sabse bada jhooth: "paisa bachane se ameer bante hain."
BODY: Bachat zaroori hai, par sirf bachat se paisa nahi badhta. Mehngai har saal uski value kam karti hai. Isliye bachat ke saath seekho ki paisa kaam pe kaise lagta hai: skill, side income, aur samajhdaari se invest.
CTA: Agree karte ho? Comment mein "haan" likho.

Total: ~25 second. Screen pe har line ke 3–5 bade shabd.

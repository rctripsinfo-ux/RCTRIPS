02 · Niche

The 5-question Niche Checklist

1Interest: could I make 100 reels on this without getting bored?
2Proof of concept: can I find 3+ faceless pages in this niche growing right now?
3Money: will this audience pay for something later (PDF, course, product, brand deals)?
4Audience quality: do people here want to learn or improve, not just laugh and scroll?
5Feasibility: can I make this content with text, voice and simple visuals, without complex animation?
Rule: all 5 must be "yes". Missing proof of concept is the most common mistake: an interesting topic with no successful pages usually means no audience on Instagram.
How to check proof: search the niche keyword on Instagram, open 3 pages, and check whether their recent reels (last 30 days) are getting views far above their follower count.

04 · Branding

Branding that converts

Name: short, easy to spell, says the niche. "@paisa.rules" beats "@the_real_finance_guy_2026".
Profile picture: faceless is fine, but it must be recognisable at tiny size: a mascot, a bold logo letter, or one icon on a strong colour. Our red-yellow mascot works because it is the same everywhere.
Bio formula: who it's for + what they get + proof/journey + one action.
Never beg: "Follow me for more" gives no reason. Give a reason instead: "Roz 1 money rule jo school ne nahi sikhaya."
Highlights: 3–5 covers in your brand colours: Start here · Best reels · Free stuff · Results.
Category: pick "Digital creator" or "Education", not a random business category.

05 · Warm-up

The first 7 days: warm up

1Act like a normal user. No mass liking, mass following or copy-paste comments. Bot-like behaviour can limit a new page.
2Train your feed. Watch, like and save only reels from your niche for 15–20 minutes a day, so Instagram learns what your page is about and shows you research material.
3Finish your profile (name, picture, bio, link, category) before the first post.
4Make 5 reels before posting the first one. A buffer protects your consistency when life gets busy.
5Switch to a Creator account so you get Insights. Instagram shows more detailed professional insights once you reach 100 followers.

06 · Research

Viral research: where to look

1Fastest-growing pages: list 10 pages in your niche in a sheet. Sort by how fast they are growing, not by total followers.
2Their following list: big pages usually follow other strong pages in the same niche. Free research.
3Intentional scrolling: after the warm-up, your Reels feed shows niche content. Scroll with a notebook, not for fun.
4Meta Ad Library: ads running for months are messages that are proven to work.
The outlier rule: a reel is worth studying only if it got 3× or more that page's usual views. A page with 10K average views and one reel at 200K = an outlier. That reel shows what the audience really wants.
Quality filter: likes around 2–5% of views, real comments (not just emojis), and posted recently (ideally within the last week). Old viral reels show what worked then, not now.

07 · Remodel

Remodel, don't copy

Taking the idea that works is learning. Re-uploading someone's reel is theft, and it gets pages removed.

KeepChange
The concept / topicThe hook wording (write 3 new ones)
The structure (list, story, myth…)Examples, numbers and language (make it Hinglish, make it Indian)
The length and paceDesign, font, colours: your signature style
Voice: your own recording or your own AI voice, never their audio
Mix sources: take ideas from many creators, never only one. A page that copies one creator gets called out, and its audience never trusts it.
Music: use Instagram's licensed library or royalty-free tracks. Copyrighted songs added in an editor can get your reel muted.

08 · Recall

Your signature style

If someone sees your reel twice, would they know it is yours? If not, every view is a stranger again.

1Same visual identity: 1 font, 2 colours, same layout for hook text.
2A signature opening or closing line (example: ending every reel with "Ab jao, kaam karo.").
3A recurring series: "Kadwa Sach Monday", "Sunday Recap".
4Same mascot / logo position in every reel.
Give it 3–6 months. Style forms slowly. Experiment in month 1, pick what works, then keep it fixed. Don't copy another creator's style: people will say you copied them.

10 · Growth

Organic growth tactics

1Early valuable comments: turn on notifications for 2–3 bigger pages (100K–500K) in your niche and leave a genuinely useful first comment. Good comments get seen by thousands. No spam, no fake accounts.
2Collabs: use Instagram's Collab feature with a similar-size page in your niche. Both audiences see the reel.
3Trial Reels: post variations (different hook or font) of the same reel to non-followers first and keep the winner.
4Reply to every comment in the first hour. Conversation signals the reel is worth pushing.
5Ride trends that fit your niche: budget day, exam season, New Year, a viral event, with your niche's angle.
The viral window: creators commonly see that after a reel goes viral, the next 3–14 days give new posts extra reach. Don't go quiet. Post more often (if possible 2/day) and post follow-ups on the same topic.

11 · Conversion

Views → followers: the real game

The example: a creator who grew from 1K to 800K followers in 150 days shared that two of his reels got almost the same views (2.1M and 2.2M), but one brought 40,000 followers and the other only 16,000. The difference: how much real value the reel gave.
Our case: 17,000 views → 116 followers (0.7%). Mostly tool lists people could save without needing us again.

How to raise conversion

1Give value that makes people want the next reel too (series, part 1/2/3).
2Talk to one person, like a friend, not to an audience.
3End with a line that hits the viewer personally, so they feel 'this page gets me'.
4Make the profile instantly explain what they'll get by following.

Mindset

Why most pages quit

The valley of disappointment: results always come slower than expected. Effort goes in, numbers stay flat, and that gap is where people quit. Results compound later, which is why most people never see them.
Shiny object syndrome: switching niche every 2 weeks resets everything. Give one niche 90 days of consistent posting before judging it.
Measure inputs, not just outputs: "Did I post today and study 3 outliers?" is in your control. Views are not.

Consistency with quality beats talent. Every time.

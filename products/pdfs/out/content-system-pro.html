03 · Structure

Har reel ka 4-part structure

SecondPartKya karna hai
0–3HOOKFrame 0 pe bada text + wahi line bolo. Intro/logo nahi.
3–8TENSIONProblem ko thoda aur khol do: 'aur isi wajah se…'
8–25VALUEPoints/kahani. Har 2–3 second mein screen pe kuch naya.
25–30PAYOFF + CTAEk yaad rehne wali line + ek action (save/comment/follow).
Shabd ginti: Hinglish mein hum ~2.5 shabd/second bolte hain, par screen text aur pauses ke saath reel lambi ho jaati hai. 50–70 shabd = 25–35 second ki reel. Isse lambi script = beech mein log chhodenge.
Ending trick: "Bye" mat bolo. Aakhri line aisi ho jo pehli line se jud jaaye, taaki reel loop ho aur watch time badhe.

05 · Convert

Kisi bhi niche mein badlo: 3 step

Step 1, format rakho, topic badlo. "5 cheezein jo strong log nahi karte" → Money: "5 cheezein jo ameer log nahi karte" → Study: "5 cheezein jo toppers nahi karte".
Step 2, apne niche ka dard daalo. Motivation ka dard: aalas. Money ka: salary khatam. Study ka: bhoolna. Facts ka: "ye mujhe pata hona chahiye tha".
Step 3, CTA niche ke hisaab se. Study → "save karo exam ke liye". Money → "is mahine ka hisaab comment karo". Facts → "kisi ko bhejo jo ye nahi jaanta".

07 · Caption

Caption formula

Line 1: hook ka doosra version (feed mein ye dikhta hai).
Line 2–4: reel ka saar, 3 short lines. Jo reel mein jaldi nikal gaya, wo yahan padh lein.
Line 5: ek sawaal ya CTA ("Aap kaunse ho? 1 ya 2").
Keywords: Instagram search caption padhta hai. 2–3 shabd daalo jo log search karte hain: "motivation hindi", "study tips", "paise kaise bachaye".
Hashtags: 3–5, sirf niche wale. 30 hashtags spam lagte hain.
Example: Aap aalsi nahi ho, aapka plan galat hai. 👇
Motivation kaam se aati hai, pehle nahi. Aaj sirf 10 minute shuru karo. Baaki dimaag khud karega.
Aaj kya shuru kar rahe ho? Comment karo.

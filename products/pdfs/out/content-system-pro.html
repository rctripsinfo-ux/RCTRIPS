03 · Structure

The 4-part structure of every reel

SecondsPartWhat to do
0–3HOOKBig text on frame 0 + say the same line. No intro or logo.
3–8TENSIONOpen the problem a little more: 'and that is exactly why…'
8–25VALUEPoints or story. Something new on screen every 2–3 seconds.
25–30PAYOFF + CTAOne memorable line + one action (save/comment/follow).
Word count: we speak about 2.5 words per second in Hinglish, but on-screen text and pauses make reels longer. 50–70 words = a 25–35 second reel. Longer scripts lose viewers in the middle.
Ending trick: don't say "Bye". Make the last line connect back to the first, so the reel loops and watch time goes up.

05 · Convert

Convert to any niche in 3 steps

Step 1, keep the format, change the topic. "5 cheezein jo strong log nahi karte" → Money: "5 cheezein jo ameer log nahi karte" → Study: "5 cheezein jo toppers nahi karte".
Step 2, swap in your niche's pain. Motivation: laziness. Money: salary runs out. Study: forgetting. Facts: "I should have known this".
Step 3, match the CTA to the niche. Study → "save karo exam ke liye". Money → "is mahine ka hisaab comment karo". Facts → "kisi ko bhejo jo ye nahi jaanta".

06 · Batch

Batch Day: one day, 7 reels

TimeTask
30 minPick the week's 7 topics (from the Weekly Format System)
60 minWrite 7 scripts (this PDF's structure, 50–70 words each)
30 minWrite 3 hooks per script, pick the best (Hook Score)

07 · Caption

Caption formula

Line 1: a second version of the hook (this is what shows in the feed).
Lines 2–4: the reel's summary in 3 short lines, so people can read what went by fast.
Line 5: a question or CTA ("Aap kaunse ho? 1 ya 2").
Keywords: Instagram search reads captions. Add 2–3 phrases people search for: "motivation hindi", "study tips", "paise kaise bachaye".
Hashtags: 3–5, niche-specific only. 30 hashtags look like spam.
Example: Aap aalsi nahi ho, aapka plan galat hai. 👇

08 · Review

Every Sunday: 15-minute review

CheckIf…Then…
ViewsA reel gets 2× your median or moreMake 3 new versions of that idea next week
Skip rate / 3-sec holdMany people leave in the first 3 secChange the hook + frame 0 (Hook Bank PRO)
Avg watch timeLess than half the reelCut the script to about 50 words
Shares/sendsOne format gets more sharesPost that format twice a week
FollowsHigh views, low followsFix your profile, bio and pinned reels
Never decide based on one reel. Use the average of the week's 7 reels. In our data, the same format ranged from 150 to 1,600 views.

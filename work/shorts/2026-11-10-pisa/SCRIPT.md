# Pisa: the tower that started falling over before it was finished

Facts (checked, cautious wording where sources differ): construction began 1173; the lean began when the third floor
went up (about 1178) on soft clay and sand; wars stopped work for almost a century; later floors were built taller on
the short side, giving the curve; 1990 lean about 5.5 degrees, tower closed; steel cables, lead counterweights and soil
extraction under the high (north) side, 1993-2001; straightened by roughly 40 cm and deliberately left leaning; studies
(Bristol / Roma Tre, 2018) credit the soft soil with helping it survive four strong earthquakes.
Sources: NOVA (PBS) Pisa interventions, Ingenia "The stabilisation of the Leaning Tower of Pisa", artnet news.

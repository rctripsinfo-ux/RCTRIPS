# The Great Emu War (Western Australia, 1932)
Facts: Nov-Dec 1932, Campion district; about 20,000 emus damaging wheat; Major G. P. W. Meredith with two soldiers,
two Lewis guns and 10,000 rounds; first ambush at a dam with about 1,000 emus, the gun jammed after about twelve birds;
a truck-mounted gun could not keep up or aim on rough ground; emus split into small groups with a lookout; withdrawn
after about a week, resumed, about 9,860 rounds for 986 claimed kills (about 10 per bird); farmers asked for help again in
later years. Meredith: the emus "can face machine guns with the invulnerability of tanks".
Sources: Wikipedia "Emu War", Britannica "Emu War", Australian Geographic "Looking back: Australia's Emu Wars".

You're curling every single day, but your arms still look like two dry noodles. Let me fix that. Here's the truth. Biceps don't make your arms look big. Triceps do. They're two thirds of your upper arm. Here are three moves that get it done. First, diamond push-ups. Hands close together, elbows tight to your ribs, chest down to your thumbs. Second, chair dips. Three sets of ten, three times a week. No more noodles. Just arms.

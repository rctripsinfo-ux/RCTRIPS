Knees hurt when you squat? Look at your knees. They cave in. That's because your side glutes are asleep. So your knees take all the load. Here's the fix. One: fire hydrants. On all fours, lift your knee out to the side, fifteen each side. Two: side leg raises. Lift your top leg slowly, fifteen each side. Then squat again, and push your knees out. Comment KNEES if yours click on the stairs.

# What the old channel's viral Shorts did (owner's files, 28 Oct)

Videos analysed (48 frames each):
- 5 Core Tricks: 7.3K views, 39 s, green H
- Triceps Mistake: 43 s, green H
- Push-Up Form: 8K views, 52 s, green H
- Dead Hangs 30 Days: 15.8K views, 66 s, realistic H

Animated clips are left out on purpose (owner).

## Text and fonts
1. **Big statement cards, typed word by word, full screen, no character.**
   - "HERE'S WHAT'S REALLY GOING ON": heavy black geometric font (Montserrat/Poppins Black style), left-aligned, 3–4 lines.
   - "FIX IS SIMPLE" and "LET'S FIX IT FAST": RED, same weight, centered.
   - "HERE ARE THE MOVES THAT MAKE IT HAPPEN": white italic with a black outline.

   So there are TWO bumpers: one before the "why" and one before the fix.
2. **Section labels.** "#1 HAND PLACEMENT", "#2 RANGE OF MOTION" and "#3 Core Control" use a red condensed font (Oswald/Bebas style). The label first appears alone in the middle of a white screen for about 0.5 s, then moves to the top while the move plays.
3. **Captions.**
   - Core, push-up and triceps videos: small bold ITALIC condensed white words with a black outline, low-middle, 1–2 words.
   - Dead hang video (the best one): ONE word at a time, big, rounded comic font (Luckiest Guy style), white with a thick black outline, in the CENTRE of the body.
4. **Muscle labels with arrows.** A red word ("Biceps", "Triceps") with a curved dashed arrow pointing at the muscle on a close-up.

## Moving the character with poses (no animation)
5. **Two-pose reps.** Push-up up/down, plank and dips swap between 2 drawings every ~0.4 s, so it reads as movement.
6. **Cross-fades between poses.** One pose ghosts into the next (half-transparent frames).
7. **Picture in picture.**
   - A big close-up H pointing at the viewer in the bottom half, with small full-body poses at the top.
   - Small → big physique poses with "-->" arrows between them (progress).
8. **A ghost H in the background.** A huge faded H (thinking or facepalm) behind the main pose.
9. **Extreme close-ups.** Face filling the screen (pain, anger), hands on the bar, shoulder on fire.

## Visual metaphors (the dead hang video had one on almost every line)
10. **Hang and core visuals:** flames along the bottom on "burning"; a muscle inside a sparkling crystal (hip flexors); a round anatomy inset with a dark background.
11. **Dead hang story visuals:**
    - timer "00:15"
    - magnifying glass ("questioning")
    - red X on the wrists
    - green arrow up ("wrap")
    - standing on anvils ("skyrocketed")
    - blue X-ray body ("decompressed")
    - height ruler ("taller")
    - a crowd filming him
    - calendar with "30"
    - smashing through an office wall
12. **Correct vs wrong guide lines.**
    - Green dashed line under the hands = right width; red dashed = wrong.
    - Red dashed angle line along the body = straight plank line.
    - Red vertical dashed line = push-up depth.
13. **Muscle glow on the working muscle.** Chest glows during push-ups, glutes go red in a bad plank, triceps catch fire in dips.
14. **Real-looking backgrounds in story scenes** (dead hang video): gym, office, bathroom, mountain, a crowd. The other videos are plain white.
15. **CTA.** A red "Subscribe" button with speed lines while H runs at the camera.

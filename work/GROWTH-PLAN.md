# Effortless Exercise: growth plan (goal: Shorts above 10K views)

Written 25 Oct 2026 from the channel export (2–24 Oct) and vidIQ outliers. Benchmarks: ONLY animated-character
fitness channels (owner, 25 Oct: no real-person workout channels).

## Where we are
- 20 Shorts, 19.7K views, 35 subs. Every Short gets ~1,000–1,600 views on day 1, then stops: none passed YouTube's first test.
- Viewers leave after ~7–14 s whatever the length: 27–30 s videos are watched 32–38%, 40–54 s videos 17–25%.
- Best topics: form mistakes and body problems (dead hang, skinny fat, push-up mistake, double push-ups, thick arms).
  Weakest: motivation and myths (always tomorrow, sweat, box body, crunches, jawline).
- Days without an upload: 20–60 channel views. Views come almost only from new uploads.

Lessons:
- **Same topics as ours** (obliques, forearms, lower back) reach 1M+ there and ~1K here, so the problem is the
  video, not the topic.
- **Short:** 14–36 s. **One muscle, one idea**, shown by the character doing the move from the first second.
- **Title = one short question or promise:** "Want thicker, stronger forearms?", "Before pull-ups, you should...".
- **Routine series** (FitVision 3D "X/365 days") is the biggest breakout for a small animated channel.

## The plan
1. **Two formats, tested side by side for 2 weeks:**
   - **A. Routine Shorts (new):** H does 4–6 home exercises (our clips), name + "3x15" per move, music,
     20–30 s, numbered series "Day 1/365 Home Workout", end loops into the start.
   - **B. One-muscle explainers (current style, shorter):** 20–30 s, 60–75 words, question hook
     ("Want thicker forearms?"), H doing the move from frame 1, red/green muscle, 1–3 moves,
     cover 0.1 s then straight into the hook (no long thumbnail opener).
2. **2 uploads a day** (1 A + 1 B) for 14 days, same times every day.
3. Every Monday: export the channel analytics (same zip) → I compare A vs B and double down on the winner.
4. Thumbnails: brush style (only matter for search/browse, not the Shorts feed).

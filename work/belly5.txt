Lower belly sticking out? It's not always fat. Here's what's really going on. Sitting all day tightens your hip flexors, they tip your pelvis forward, and your belly gets pushed out. Quick test: stand with your back against a wall. Big gap behind your lower back? That's the tilt. Here's the fix. One: hip flexor stretch, thirty seconds each side. Two: glute bridges, fifteen reps. Three: dead bugs, ten slow reps. Do it daily, and in two weeks you stand taller. Comment HIPS if your belly just got exposed.

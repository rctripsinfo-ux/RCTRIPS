Meet H. For one whole year, he did curls. Morning curls. Night curls. Hundreds of curls, every single day. Then one day, he flexed in the mirror, and froze. His arms looked exactly the same. He almost quit. Then an old coach walked over and said: Your biceps are only one third of your arm. The other two thirds are triceps. H had never trained them. Not once. So he swapped half his curls for chair dips and diamond push-ups. Three sets. Slow on the way down. Three months later, his sleeves got tight. Same effort. Smarter plan.

Your push-ups are not working? You are making these three mistakes. Here's the fix. One: your hips drop. Keep your body straight, like a plank of wood. Two: your hands are too wide. Put them right under your shoulders. Three: you only go half way. Bring your chest close to the floor. Do three sets of ten, every other day. Ten good push-ups beat fifty bad ones. Comment PUSH if you just found your mistake.

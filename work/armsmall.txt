You've done curls every day for a year, and your sleeves still fit the same. Here's what nobody tells you. Your biceps are only one third of your arm. The other two thirds are triceps. So train them. Number one, diamond push-ups. Hands close, elbows tight to your ribs. Number two, chair dips. Elbows straight back, lower to ninety degrees. Number three, pike push-ups. Hips high, head between your hands. Three sets of ten, three times a week. Send this to the friend who only does curls.

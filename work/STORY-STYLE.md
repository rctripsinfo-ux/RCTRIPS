# Storytelling style (from 22 Oct, sample: final/smallarms.mp4)

Owner's brief for all future videos. Builder template: `build_smallarms.py`.

## Script
- A short story with one character (H): hook in the first line ("Meet H."), the struggle, the turning point
  (someone tells him the truth), the fix, the result after a time skip, then "Send this to the friend who …".
- 35–40 s, ~100–110 words, short sentences so the pictures can change on every line.

## Visuals
- The story is told by pictures: a new H pose or clip on every line (every 1–3 s), chosen to act out the sentence
  (alarm for "morning", sleepy for "night", selfie for "mirror", despair for "almost quit", walk-in for a time skip).
- Exercise moments use the animation clips (`clip_*`), clip scenes on white.
- Slow push-in (`motion: zoomIn`) on still poses, camera punch on the shock moment, impact frames on 2 words max.
- Backgrounds carry the mood: yellow spotlight = everyday / confusion, red = low point, green = the truth / result,
  rays = CTA. Never the same colour two scenes in a row.

## Text (minimal)
- No word captions (`captions.mode: "none"`).
- At most 3 on-screen texts in the whole video, only numbers or one word that is the key idea
  (sample: "DAY 1→365" counter, "1/3", "2/3").
- No tapes, marquees, echo stacks or labels.

## Sound
- Voice clearly on top. Music at volume 0.09 (about 11 dB under the voice), sound effects at sfxVolume 0.3
  (quieter than the music on average). No meme voice clips that talk over the voice.
- Soft sounds only: whooshes, pops, camera, sparkle, ding, magic, gold bell; ambience (wind) for the low point.
- Final file normalised to -14 LUFS (YouTube Shorts level), true peak -1.5 dB, video stream copied.

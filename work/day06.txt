You do bicep curls every single day, but your arms still look small in your T-shirt. You're working hard, so why aren't they growing? Here's the secret most people miss.

Your bicep is the muscle on the front of your arm. Your tricep is on the back, and it's actually bigger. It makes up about two-thirds of your upper arm. So if you only train biceps, you're skipping the biggest part of your arm.

Here are three easy moves for your triceps. First, close-grip push-ups. Put your hands close together under your chest and keep your elbows tucked in. Do ten. Second, bench dips. Put your hands on a chair behind you, bend your elbows to lower yourself, then push back up. Do twelve. Third, overhead extensions. Hold one dumbbell or a water bottle with both hands above your head, lower it behind your head, then lift it back up. Do twelve.

Do these twice a week along with your curls, and your arms will start to look fuller. Share this with a friend who only trains biceps.

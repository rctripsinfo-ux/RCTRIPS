Stop curling. Seventy percent of your arm is triceps. Biceps are the small part in front. Triceps are the big part at the back. Curls only, and your arms stay small. Here's the fix. One: bench dips. Hands on a bench, lower slowly, twelve reps. Two: diamond push-ups. Hands close, elbows back, ten reps. Three: overhead extensions. Lower the weight behind your head, twelve reps. Train these twice a week, and your arms finally look big. Comment ARMS if you only did curls.

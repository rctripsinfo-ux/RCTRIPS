Can't do a single pull-up? Your back muscles don't know how to pull yet. Here's the fix. One: dead hang. Thirty seconds, to build your grip. Two: scapula pulls. Hang, then pull your shoulders down, ten reps. Three: negatives. Jump to the top, then lower slowly for five seconds. Do this three times a week, and your first pull-up comes in a few weeks.

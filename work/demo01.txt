You grab the bar. You pull with everything you've got. And your chin... stays exactly where it was. Here's the fix.

Most people pull with their arms only. But a pull-up starts in your back. If your back never switches on, your arms are lifting your whole body alone. No wonder they quit.

Three steps. One: dead hangs. Just hang with straight arms for thirty seconds. That builds your grip. Two: scap pulls. Stay hanging and pull your shoulders down, without bending your arms. Do ten. Three: negatives. Jump to the top, then lower yourself as slowly as you can. Do five.

Train this three days a week, and in a month that bar won't stand a chance. What's your max right now? Comment it below.

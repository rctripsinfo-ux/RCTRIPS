M: So should people do it or not?
H: Do it. But do the smarter version. Three rules.
H: Rule one. Split the reps across the day. Never grind them out in one ugly set.
H: Rule two. Take one easy day a week. Fifty push-ups instead of a hundred. Joints need recovery too.
H: Rule three. After two weeks, make it harder. Slow the way down to three seconds, or raise your feet on a chair.
M: And if you can't do one push-up?
H: Then start on your knees, or with your hands on a wall or a table. Same muscles, less weight. A hundred wall push-ups still counts.

H: Week two, something strange happens. The hundred gets faster. The sets get bigger. Twenty, twenty-five in a row.
M: Wait, did you grow muscle that fast?
H: Not really. Most of the early strength comes from the brain and the nerves, not new muscle.
H: Your nervous system learns the movement. It switches on more muscle fibres, at the same time, more smoothly.
M: So your muscles didn't get bigger. They just got... organised.
H: Exactly. Same team, better coordination. Muscle growth you can actually see usually takes more like six to eight weeks.

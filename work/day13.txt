Sitting eight hours a day shortens your hips. Your hip flexors get tight, and your lower back may start to ache. Even standing up feels stiff. Here's the fix. One: hip flexor stretch, thirty seconds each side. Two: pigeon stretch, thirty seconds each side. Three: lying spinal twist, thirty seconds each side. Five minutes after work, and your hips feel free. Comment HIPS if you sit all day.

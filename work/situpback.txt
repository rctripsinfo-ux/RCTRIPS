Every sit-up you do is pulling on your lower back, not building your abs. Here's what's actually happening. When you sit all the way up, your hip flexors do the lifting, and your lower back arches off the floor. Want to fix it? First, flatten your lower back. Lie down, knees bent, and press your back into the floor. Hold that every rep. Second, swap sit-ups for planks. Squeeze your glutes and keep one straight line. Third, reverse crunches. Curl your hips up slowly, don't swing. Three sets, three times a week. Send this to the friend who does a hundred sit-ups a day.

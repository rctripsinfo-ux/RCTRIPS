You're doing push-ups every single day, but from behind you still look like a fridge?

Here's the fix.

Your chest doesn't make you look wide. Your back does. And if you want that V-shape, you need to train your lats and your upper back.

Here are three moves that get it done.

First, inverted rows. A table, a bar, a broomstick across two chairs, it doesn't matter. Keep your body straight, and pull your chest to the bar.

Second, pull-ups. Perfect for building width without any machine. Hands wide, chest up, pull your elbows down to your ribs. Can't do one yet? Jump up and lower yourself slowly. Slow and brutal, in a good way.

Third, dead hangs. This one stretches your lats and builds your grip. Arms straight, shoulders down, hold for thirty seconds.

These three back moves will turn that fridge into a V. Which one are you skipping?

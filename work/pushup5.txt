Push-ups build a strong upper body, but only if you do them right. Odds are, you're making at least one of these five mistakes. Mistake one: looking up. It bends your neck out of line. Look at the floor just ahead of your hands. Mistake two: elbows flared out like a T. That puts your shoulders at risk. Tuck them to forty-five degrees, like an arrow. Mistake three: sagging hips. That crushes your lower back. Squeeze your glutes and keep one straight line. Mistake four: shoulders rolled forward. Your chest switches off. Pull them back and stay tight. Mistake five: going straight up and down. Push the floor away, slightly forward and up. Send this to the friend whose push-ups need fixing.

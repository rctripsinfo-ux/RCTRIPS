You finish a long day on your phone or laptop, and your neck feels stiff and heavy. Your shoulders are tight, and you keep rolling your head to get some relief. If that sounds like you, you’re not alone, and there’s a simple reason it happens.

Your head weighs about five kilos. When you tilt it forward to look down at a screen, your neck muscles have to work much harder to hold it up. Research suggests a deep forward tilt can feel like many times that weight. Do that for hours every day and your neck gets tired and sore.

Here are three easy moves to fix it. First, chin tucks. Sit tall and gently slide your chin straight back, like you’re making a double chin. Hold for three seconds and do ten. Second, the doorway stretch. Put your forearms on a door frame and step forward slowly until you feel your chest open. Hold for twenty seconds. Third, wall angels. Stand with your back against a wall and slowly slide your arms up and down, ten times.

Do these every evening, and lift your phone to eye level during the day. Your neck will thank you. If the pain is sharp or doesn’t go away, see a doctor.

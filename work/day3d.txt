Can you hang from a bar for sixty seconds? Most people drop before thirty. Let's test you. Under twenty seconds? Weak grip. Thirty seconds? Average. Sixty seconds? Strong. Ninety plus? Beast mode. Dropped early? It's not your arms. It's your grip. Your forearms get tired first, and your hands slide off like butter. Here's the fix. One: dead hangs. Hang as long as you can, three times. Two: add five seconds every day. Thirty becomes sixty in a few weeks. Do this and your grip gets crazy strong. Now comment your time, and be honest.

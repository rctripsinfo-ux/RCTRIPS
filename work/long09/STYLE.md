# Yellow Dude style (measured with rea-video, JuneYaooo/rea-video, on their long videos; L01 fully broken down, rest batch)
- ONE plain background colour for the whole video (theirs: pale periwinkle). No room/garage backdrops. All focus on the character + the gag.
- Something new on screen every 0.3-1 s (rea-video "visual change" keyframes): props / icons pop in and out per phrase,
  the character changes pose, the camera punches in. Our long06/07/08: ~60% of frames still vs theirs ~35%.
- Every spoken line is illustrated by an ICON or a PROP GAG (snooze button, phone, wifi, hotel sign, plane, emoji, red-X circle inset), not by words.
- Very little on-screen text: only exercise title cards ("#1 CRUCIFIX CRUNCH" white on dark slate) and a few single words.
- Shot sizes change all the time: huge close-ups (face / hand fills the frame), big half-body, small full body. Motion-blur entries.
- Repeat-gag in quick cuts in the hook (snooze slammed 10x in 0.4 s cuts).
- Exercise demos are moving loops held 10-45 s with a small red-X / tick circle inset for form tips.
- Sound: TTS male narrator, upbeat library music ~185 BPM, ~13 SFX per minute (ding on every new element, pop, whoosh), comic FX (POW burst, lightning).
- Ends with a punch to camera / subscribe gag.

Keep doing leg raises like that, and you'll get zero abs, back pain, and maybe a new chiropractor. Here's what's actually happening. You swing your legs up fast and feel a burn. That burn is not your abs. It's your hip flexors, pulling on your lower back. Want to fix it? Press your lower back into the floor. Bend your knees a little. Lower your legs slowly, and stop before your back lifts. Slow and controlled, every single rep. Master this first, then level up.

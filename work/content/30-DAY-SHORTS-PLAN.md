## 3. Hook rules used in every script
1. **The first 1.5 s carries a picture and a contradiction**, e.g. "You train hard. The mirror disagrees." No "Hey guys", no intro.
2. **Mini-H interrupts before 4 s.** A second voice resets attention.
3. **An open loop in the first 5 s**: "number 3 is the one nobody does", "the last test is brutal".
4. **A pattern change every 3–4 s**: marker, sticky note, meter, lens, page turn.
5. **The payoff answers the hook exactly**, and Mini-H's last line is a comment question.

## 4. Scripts (≈ 45–55 s each, ~110–125 words)

### Day 1: The Laziest Way to Build Muscle
H: You don't need two hours in the gym. You need forty minutes, three times a week.
M: That's it? I was promised suffering.
H: Lazy rule one. Full body, three days a week. Every muscle gets hit three times instead of once.
H: Rule two. Fewer sets, but hard ones. Stop when you have one or two reps left in the tank.
M: So… quit early?
H: Quit smart. Rule three. Add one rep or a little weight every week. That's it. That's the whole secret.
[Effort-o-meter: EFFORT low, RESULT high]
H: Forty minutes. Three days. One more rep each week.
M: Okay, which day are you starting? Tell me below.

### Day 2: How Many Push-Ups Should You Do at Your Age?
H: Drop and count. Your age decides if your number is weak or strong.
M: Please don't say it out loud.
H: [sticky notes per age] In your twenties, around thirty clean push-ups is solid. Thirties, around twenty-five. Forties, twenty. Fifties, fifteen. Sixty plus, ten is already great.
H: Clean means chest close to the floor and body straight like a plank.
M: What if I did eleven… at twenty-two?
H: Then do three sets of what you can, every other day. In a month, that number moves.
H: These are rough benchmarks, not a test of your worth.
M: Your age and your number. Comment it. No lying.

### Day 3: What Actually Makes Muscles Grow
H: Sweat doesn't build muscle. Soreness doesn't either. One thing does.
M: Protein shakes?
H: [X-Ray lens into the arm] Tension. When a muscle works hard close to its limit, its fibres get a signal: grow, or this happens again.
H: Then two things finish the job. Protein, about one point six grams per kilo of body weight a day. And sleep, because that's when the repair happens.
M: So the gym is just… the order form?
H: Exactly. The gym places the order. Food and sleep deliver it.
H: Hard sets. Enough protein. Seven to nine hours.
M: Which one are you skipping? Be honest below.

### Day 4: Be Honest, What's Your Arm Level?
H: Every arm on Earth fits one of five levels. Find yours.
M: Can we skip the first one?
H: [conveyor of 5 arms, marker numbers] Level one, the noodle. Level two, the beginner bump. Level three, the sleeve-filler. Level four, the shirt-stretcher. Level five, the doorframe problem.
M: I'm at one point five.
H: Most guys get stuck at three because they only curl. The back of the arm is two-thirds of the size. Train both, and add one rep a week.
H: Levels change. Mirrors don't lie, but they do update.
M: What level are you right now? Comment the number.

### Day 5: The 10-Minute Walk That Burns More Than You Think
H: You just ate. Don't sit. This is the laziest fat-loss trick there is.
M: Walking? That's not exercise.
H: [meter swings] A ten-minute walk right after a meal helps your muscles soak up the sugar from that meal, so your blood sugar spikes less.
H: Do it after your biggest meal every day. That's seventy extra minutes of moving every week, without a single workout.
M: Can I walk to the fridge?
H: You can walk past it.
H: Ten minutes. After meals. Every day.
M: Which meal are you walking after? Tell me below.

### Day 6: Why Tendons Take Forever to Heal
H: Your muscle healed in a week. Your elbow still hurts after three months. Here's why.
M: Because I'm old?
H: [X-Ray lens: muscle red and bright, tendon pale] Muscles have a big blood supply. Tendons have very little. Less blood means slower repair.
H: And resting completely makes it worse. Tendons need load to rebuild.
M: So… the answer is pain?
H: The answer is gentle holds. Push or pull against something for thirty to forty-five seconds, at a level that feels manageable. Five holds a day.
H: Slow and steady loads rebuild tendons. If the pain is sharp or getting worse, see a physio.
M: Which joint hurts? Tell me below.

### Day 7: 5 Boring Habits Killing Your Testosterone
H: Your testosterone isn't low because of bad luck. It's these five boring habits.
M: Boring? I'm boring. Am I doomed?
H: One. Sleeping five hours. Even one week of short sleep can drop it noticeably.
H: Two. Never lifting anything heavy. Three. Carrying extra belly fat. Four. Drinking most weekends.
M: That's my whole personality.
H: Five. Sitting all day, no sun, no walking.
H: The fix is just as boring. Sleep seven to nine hours. Lift three times a week. Walk outside daily.
M: Which habit are you guilty of? Number in the comments.

### Day 8: Your Birth Month Is Your Fitness Challenge

M: I was born in a leap year. I'm exempt.
H: [calendar page, sticky per month] January, thirty squats. February, a one-minute plank. March, twenty push-ups. April, fifty jumping jacks. May, a one-minute wall sit. June, twenty lunges.
H: July, thirty-second dead bug. August, fifteen burpees. September, forty mountain climbers. October, twenty glute bridges. November, a two-minute walk on your toes. December, all of the above.
M: December people, I'm so sorry.
H: Do it now, then comment your month and "done".

### Day 9: How Many Ab Exercises Do You Really Need?
H: Ten ab exercises a day, and still no abs? You need three. Max.
M: But the more I suffer, the better… right?
H: [three pages, lens on the core] Your core does three jobs. Bend, like a crunch. Stop your back from arching, like a dead bug. And stop your body from twisting, like a side plank.
H: One exercise for each job. Three sets, twice a week. Done.
M: And the six-pack shows up?
H: It shows up when body fat comes down. Training builds it. Eating reveals it.
M: Which job is your core worst at? Tell me below.

### Day 10: Look 2 Inches Taller in 5 Minutes

M: Wait, I can grow?
H: [Mirror Check: slouched reflection] No. But you can stop shrinking. A rounded back and forward head can make you look up to two inches shorter.
H: Fix one. Wall angels, ten slow reps. Fix two. Chin tucks, ten reps. Fix three. A doorway chest stretch, thirty seconds.
H: [mirror: tall reflection] Shoulders back, chest up, head stacked. Same body, more height.
M: I gained two inches, and I didn't even eat my vegetables.
M: Do you slouch at your desk? Be honest below.

### Day 11: How Strong Are You Really? Four Tests

H: Test three. A one-minute plank with no sagging. Test four. Hang from a bar for thirty seconds.

### Day 12: Abs Without Leaving Your Bed
H: Too tired to work out? Fine. Stay in bed. We train here.
M: Finally, a workout for my lifestyle.
H: [bed page] Move one. Dead bug. Lie on your back, arms up, lower opposite arm and leg slowly. Ten each side.

### Day 13: The #1 Recovery Mistake (It's Your Pillow)
H: You train hard, you eat well, and your muscles still won't grow. Check your pillow.
M: My pillow is perfect. I use it for eight minutes a night.

### Day 15: Get a Chair. Watch This.

### Day 16: How Long Should You Rest Between Sets?
H: You rest forty-five seconds to stay "intense". You might be shrinking your gains.
M: I rest for four Instagram reels.
H: [timer sticky] For big lifts like squats, rows, or push-ups, research shows two to three minutes of rest lets you do more reps in the next set. More reps, more growth.
H: For small exercises like curls, sixty to ninety seconds is plenty.
M: So I'm allowed to rest longer?

M: How long do you usually rest? Be honest below.

## 5. Notes
- **Safety lines are kept on purpose** (tendons, benchmarks, body fat): rough guides, see a physio for sharp pain.
- **Every number in the scripts follows mainstream sports-science guidance:**
  - protein ~1.6 g/kg
  - 2–3 min rest for compound lifts
  - post-meal walks blunt glucose spikes
  - short sleep lowers testosterone
  - muscles grow from tension close to failure
  - isometrics for tendon pain
- **Production per video:** script → H + Mini-H voices → `shorts-director` skill → stills check → render → `tools/edgecheck.py`.
- **New props needed:** chair, bed, backpack, calendar page and water jugs (Flow prompts on request).

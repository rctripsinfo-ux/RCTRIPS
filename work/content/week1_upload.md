## Day 6: `final/day06_upload.mp4`
**Title:** Why Tendon Pain Takes Forever to Heal

**Description**
```
Your muscle healed in a week, but your elbow still hurts after three months? Muscles have a big blood supply; tendons have very little, so they repair slowly. Full rest can make it worse, because tendons need load to rebuild.

✅ Gentle holds: push or pull against something for 30–45 seconds
✅ At a level that feels manageable
✅ 5 holds a day

Sharp or worsening pain? See a physio. Which joint hurts? 👇
```

## Description
You don't need 2 hours in the gym to build muscle. You need 40 minutes, 3 times a week.

The 3 lazy rules that actually work:
✅ Train full body 3 days a week, so every muscle gets hit 3 times instead of once
✅ Do fewer sets, but make them hard: stop with 1–2 reps left in the tank
✅ Add one rep or a little weight every week (progressive overload)

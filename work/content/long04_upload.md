The smarter version:
✅ Split the reps across the day. Never grind one ugly set.
✅ One easy day a week (50 instead of 100). Joints recover slower than muscles.
✅ After 2 weeks, make it harder: 3 seconds down, or feet on a chair.
Can't do one yet? Knees, or a wall. Same muscles, less weight.

Study mentioned: Kikuchi & Nakazato (2017), Journal of Exercise Science & Fitness: low-load bench press and push-up training produced similar muscle growth.
The numbers (22 → 38) are a typical example; your results will differ. Wrist, elbow or shoulder pain that gets worse? Stop and see a professional.

## HOOK
> kitchen, H scoops white powder into a shaker; calendar on the wall flips 12 pages fast
H: For one year, I took five grams of creatine every single day. Three hundred sixty-five scoops.
> counter 0 -> 365
H: Everyone warned me. You'll get fat. You'll bloat. You'll wreck your kidneys. And you'll lose your hair.
> four red warning stickers slap onto the tub one by one: FAT, BLOAT, KIDNEYS, HAIR
H: So I tracked everything. My weight, my strength, my blood tests. And yes, my hair.
> H points at his bald shiny head, deadpan
M: You don't have any hair.
H: That's why I was the perfect test subject. Stay till the end for that one.

## WHAT IT ACTUALLY IS
> card WHAT IS IT?
H: First, what is creatine? It's not a steroid. It's a compound your body already makes, and it's in red meat and fish.
> steak and fish icons flow into H's body
H: Your muscles use it to recharge their energy for short, hard efforts. Think of it as a tiny battery for every heavy rep.
> X-ray muscle: small batteries refill from red to green after each rep
H: Most people only have their batteries about two-thirds full. Five grams a day tops them up. That's it.
> battery 2/3 -> full

## MONTH 1: THE SCALE
> calendar page MONTH 1
H: Month one. I stepped on the scale and I was one and a half kilos heavier.
> bathroom scale needle jumps, H shocked
H: My first thought: it's making me fat. It wasn't.
H: Creatine pulls water into your muscle cells, not under your skin. So the muscle looks fuller, not puffy.
> X-ray muscle cell like a water balloon filling up | "WATER IN THE MUSCLE, NOT FAT"
H: And no bloated belly. That only happens with huge loading doses. I just took five grams with breakfast.
> H with coffee and shaker, flat belly, tick

## MONTH 3: THE REPS
> calendar page MONTH 3
H: Month three, the gym changed. Not a lot. But every set, I got one or two extra reps.
> H bench / push-ups, rep counter 8 -> 10
H: Those extra reps add up. More reps every week means more work, and more work means more muscle over time.
> stacked bars week by week growing a little each time
M: So creatine doesn't build muscle by itself?
H: No. It lets you train a little harder. You still have to show up and do the work.
> H lifting, sweat

## MONTH 6: THE BLOOD TEST
> calendar page MONTH 6
H: Month six. Time to check the scary one. My kidneys.
> H at a clinic, blood test, nervous face
H: My creatinine came back a bit high, and for one second I panicked.
> lab report with one value in red
H: Then my doctor explained it. Creatinine is just the waste product of creatine. Take more creatine, you make more of it. It doesn't mean your kidneys are damaged.
> the red value turns green | "NORMAL WITH CREATINE"
H: In healthy people, studies over years show no kidney damage at normal doses. If you already have kidney problems, talk to your doctor first.

## MONTH 12: THE RESULTS
> calendar page MONTH 12
H: One full year. Here's everything that changed.
> H stands in front of the calendar, all pages torn
H: My weight went up two kilos and most of it stayed as muscle and water in the muscle.
> scale 2 kg, arm looks fuller
H: My bench press went up more than my friend's, and we trained together, same plan.
> Kabir and H at the bench, H's bar slightly heavier
H: My energy in long sets was better. And on low-sleep days, my head felt a bit sharper. There's early research on that, too.
> brain glow, sleepy-eyes then alert
H: What didn't change? No fat gain. No bloat. No stomach problems. And I never needed a loading phase.

## THE HAIR QUESTION
> card THE HAIR QUESTION
H: Now, the hair. This myth comes from one small study in rugby players, where one hormone went up.
> one small paper, magnifying glass, "1 STUDY"
H: Nobody actually measured hair loss in that study, and newer studies haven't found the same effect.
> paper gets a question mark; newer papers stack up with ticks
H: So creatine is very unlikely to make you lose your hair.
> H rubs his bald head, deadpan
H: As for me, I'll let you be the judge.
> H's shiny head reflects a light sparkle; Kabir laughing

## HOW TO TAKE IT
> card HOW TO TAKE IT
H: If you want to try it, it's simple. Plain creatine monohydrate. Three to five grams a day, every day, any time.
> tub "MONOHYDRATE", scoop, 3-5 g
H: Mix it in water, juice or your coffee. No fancy versions, no loading, no cycling.
> shaker, coffee, X on fancy bottles
H: And drink enough water.

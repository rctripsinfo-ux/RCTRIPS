Doing hundreds of push-ups that look tough, but your chest still looks flat? Here's the fix. You only need three small form fixes, and your chest finally starts to grow. Number one: hand placement. Most people put their hands too wide or too close. Keep your hands just outside your shoulders, right under your chest. Now your chest, shoulders and triceps all work together. Number two: range of motion. Half reps give you half results. Go down until your chest almost touches the floor, then push back up with power. And keep your elbows in. Don't flap them out like chicken wings. Number three: core control. If your hips sag or pop up, that's not a push-up, that's bad yoga. Keep one straight line from your head to your heels, like a plank that moves. That's it. Three small fixes, one big difference. No gym, no equipment. Just clean reps and a real chest.
